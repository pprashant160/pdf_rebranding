Guidely PDF Cover & Branding Studio

Bulk process exam PDFs from Google Sheets or select a few PDFs manually. Typography, watermark sizing and high-resolution cover rendering stay in the app.

Inner-page watermark cleanup: Bulk mode supports per-row cover settings from Google Sheet/CSV. Inner pages are rasterized, pale cyan/light watermark pixels are cleaned, then one diagonal Guidely watermark is added. This can affect other very light cyan artwork and makes inner-page text non-selectable. Test one PDF first and inspect every page before batch use.
